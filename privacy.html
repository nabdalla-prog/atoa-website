Your privacy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Your tax papers hold some of your most private information: your Social Security number, your income, your family. This page explains, in plain words, what we do with it.

What this website collects

Nothing that identifies you. The site has no forms, no accounts and no advertising trackers. It remembers two small settings on your own device (light or dark mode, and whether you closed the language bar), and they never leave your device. Like any website, the companies that deliver it see basic technical details such as your IP address: GitHub hosts the site and Google provides its fonts. We do not receive or use that information.

When you contact us

You can reach us by WhatsApp, phone, email or in person. We use what you send only to answer you and to prepare your tax return. WhatsApp messages are encrypted from end to end, but regular email is not always. For your Social Security number and tax documents, the safest choice is to bring them in person, or ask us first about the best way to send them.

How we use your information

Only to prepare and file your tax return and to contact you about it. When we file, your return goes to the IRS and your state tax agency, as you authorize.

We never sell or share it

Federal law (section 7216 of the Internal Revenue Code) forbids tax preparers from using or sharing your tax information for any other purpose without your written consent. We do not sell your information, and we never will.

How we protect it

Federal rules (the FTC Safeguards Rule) require tax preparers to protect their clients' information. Only the people preparing your return see your documents, and you get a copy of everything you sign.

Questions about your information?

Ask us at any time what information we have about you, or for copies of your documents.
