Part I · Services

Taxes for people first. Businesses too.

Most of our clients are individuals and families. Whatever your situation, we've likely seen it before, and we'll explain it in the language you're most comfortable with.

1040

Personal & family returns

Federal and state returns for workers, families, students and retirees, with every credit you qualify for: child tax credit, earned income credit, education credits.

Multi‑state

Moved, or work in another state

Lived or worked in more than one state this year? We file each state return correctly so you aren't taxed twice. For example, Iowa and Illinois have a wage agreement: only the state you live in taxes your paycheck.

Sch. C

Self‑employed & gig work

Rideshare and delivery drivers, truckers, home daycare, cleaning, hair and beauty. We count your mileage and expenses so you pay tax on your profit, not on everything the app reports.

W‑7

ITIN applications

Help with Form W‑7 for a spouse or children who can't get a Social Security number, filed together with your return. We'll explain how to send your ID documents to the IRS (originals or certified copies) or where to have them checked in person.

1040‑X

Past years & corrections

Didn't file last year, or found a mistake? We prepare late and amended returns and help you get back on track.

CP · IRS

IRS & state letters

Bring the letter. We'll read it with you, explain what it means in your language, and help you answer before the deadline.

LLC · S

Small business returns

For owners of LLCs, partnerships and S corporations: the business return, quarterly estimates and the owner's personal return, all in one place.

Part V · Trust

Know who's doing your taxes.

The IRS warns about “ghost preparers”: people who charge to do your taxes, don't sign the return, and disappear when there's a problem. Newcomers and families are common targets. Here's how we work, and the warning signs to watch for anywhere.

IRS guide: how to choose a tax preparer

Our promises

  • We sign every return we prepare, with our IRS preparer number (PTIN).
  • Your refund goes only to your own bank account.
  • Our fee is never a percentage of your refund.
  • You get a copy of everything you sign.
  • We tell you the fee before we start.

Warning signs, anywhere

  • The preparer won't sign the return.
  • You're asked to sign a blank or unfinished form.
  • A big refund is promised before anyone sees your papers.
  • Your refund goes to someone else's account.
  • Cash only, and no receipt.

Part VII · Questions

What people ask us first.

Do I need to speak English?

No. You can do the whole appointment in Arabic, Swahili, French or English. The forms are in English, as the IRS requires, but we explain every line in your language.

I don't live near Rock Island. Can you still do my taxes?

Yes. We prepare federal returns and state returns for all 50 states. We meet by phone or video, you send your documents securely, and you sign electronically. Same language, same care as in the office.

I live in Illinois and work in Iowa. Do I file two state returns?

Usually just one. Iowa and Illinois have an agreement that taxes wages only in the state where you live. If the wrong state took tax from your paycheck, we file to get it back and show you how to fix it with your employer.

My spouse or child doesn't have a Social Security number.

They may be able to get an ITIN, a tax number from the IRS. We can help with the application (Form W‑7) and send it with your return. We'll explain how to send your ID documents to the IRS (originals or certified copies) or where to have them checked in person.

I drive for Uber, Lyft or DoorDash. What do I need?

The yearly summary from each app and a record of the miles you drove for work. Mileage often lowers your tax a lot, so even a rough record helps.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your return, so we tell you the fee before we start. It's never a percentage of your refund.

When will I get my refund?

The IRS says most e‑filed returns with direct deposit are refunded within 21 days. By law, refunds that include the earned income credit or the additional child tax credit can't be sent before mid‑February.

Can I bring a family member?

Of course. Many clients come with a spouse, a parent or a grown child.
